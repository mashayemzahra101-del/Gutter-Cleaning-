=== SECTION: When to Clean Your Gutters in Cartersville ===
h2: When to Clean Your Gutters in Cartersville
h3: October–November (Peak Leaf Season)
para: Debris drop hits hardest here, and homes with mature trees overhead fill faster than newer builds nearby. Leaves settle into the downspout opening before the trough itself even looks full, which is why overflow often starts before a gutter appears clogged. This is the busiest stretch of the year for cleaning calls, and booking early keeps a home off a waiting list before the holidays.
h3: December–February (Freeze Risk)
para: Cleaning calls slow down here, but a gutter left full going into a hard freeze is the riskiest setup of the year. Trapped water can freeze along the roofline, and the added weight pulls at brackets never built to hold ice. This is why a fall cleaning matters more than a winter one, prevention beats repair once temperatures drop.
h3: March–May (Storm Season)
para: Debris that survived winter, broken twigs, waterlogged leaves, combines with new spring growth, doubling the load a gutter has to handle. Spring storms test that load directly, and a system already slowed by winter debris overflows faster once heavy rain hits. This is the second busiest season of the year, right behind fall.
h3: June–September (Preventive Window)
para: Demand eases here since debris drop slows down, but a strong summer storm can still fill a clear gutter overnight. This stretch is actually the best window for a mid-year check, catching a loose bracket or a sagging section before fall's heavier load arrives. Waiting until leaves are already falling to find a problem leaves less time to fix it before winter.

=== SECTION: Signs You Need Downspout Cleaning ===
section label: Warning Signs
h2: Signs You Need Downspout Cleaning
para: Rainwater should move from the gutter to the ground without stopping. If you notice any of these, the downspout needs a look.
h3: Water Pools Near the Home
para: Water that does not drain away can pool near the foundation. Continued pooling causes damage to the foundation over time.
h3: Water Overflows From the Gutter
para: Water flowing over the gutter edge means rainwater is not moving through the system properly. A blocked downspout is a common cause of gutter overflow.
h3: Persistent Wet Ground Near the Foundation
para: A constantly soggy area near your home’s foundation indicates that water is not draining properly and may be putting the foundation at risk.
h3: Water Backs Up in the Gutter
para: When water backs up instead of flowing through the downspout, it can indicate a blockage that is preventing proper drainage.
faq question: What are the signs of a clogged downspout?
faq answer: The main signs are gutter overflow, slow water flow, visible debris, water pooling near the home, and water not reaching the expected outlet. If you notice any of these, the downspout should be inspected and cleaned.

h2: Why a Clean Gutter Can Still Overflow
para: Your gutter and downspout work as one system, but they can fail separately. A spotless gutter will still spill over if the downspout beneath it is blocked. The water has nowhere to go, so it backs up and pours over the edge. That is why we never stop at the trough. Clearing the gutter without checking the downspout leaves the job half done.
=== SECTION: What Causes Downspout Blockages ===
section label: Root Causes
h2: What Causes Downspout Blockages
para: Compacted debris is the most common culprit. Leaves and dirt settle inside the pipe, pack down over time, and harden into a plug. Damage can block flow too: a crushed or bent section restricts water even when the pipe is empty. Elbow joints are the first place trouble shows up, because the bend catches debris and holds on to it.
